Before you begin

Read each line out loud.

Wear headphones, so the microphone hears your voice and not the music.

Use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari. Firefox can't listen to your voice.

Use a laptop or desktop computer.

Allow microphone access when your browser asks.

Find a quiet room and read at a natural pace.
